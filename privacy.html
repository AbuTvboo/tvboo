TVBoo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Deutsch · Türkçe · Home

In short: TVBoo has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. All data stays on your device. The developer receives no data from you.

1. Who is responsible

TVBoo is developed by AbuTvboo.
Email: TVBoo [at] proton.me
Also: github.com/AbuTvboo/tvboo/issues

2. What TVBoo stores – on your device only

  • Playlists you add, including your provider's server address, username and password
  • Channels, categories, favorites, watchlist, recently watched, reminders and settings
  • Programme guide data (EPG) and cached channel logos
  • Recordings, if you start any (in the folder you choose)
  • A local error log if the app crashes. It never leaves the device by itself (see section 5).

This data is not sent to the developer or to third parties. You delete it by uninstalling TVBoo or by clearing the app data in the Android settings.

3. Internet connections

TVBoo only connects to addresses you set up yourself:

  • your IPTV provider's server (playlist, channels, movies, series, streams)
  • EPG addresses that come with your playlist or that you enter
  • logo addresses from your playlist or EPG
  • network shares (SMB) you set up

As with any internet request, these servers see your device's IP address. Their handling of data is the responsibility of their operators, in particular your IPTV provider.

Only in the direct version from this website (not in the Amazon Appstore or Google Play)

While the respective feature is switched on, the direct version also contacts: built-in EPG sources (epgshare01.online, iptv-epg.org), the iptv-org logo collection on GitHub, and an update address if you enter one. Only the download request itself is sent, no data about your channels or how you use the app.

4. Features in your local network

  • Setup and login via phone: The TV shows a QR code. Your phone sends playlist data or a screenshot end-to-end encrypted directly to the TV in the same Wi-Fi. Text recognition runs on the TV, without any cloud. Nothing is sent to third parties.
  • Transfer to another device: Data goes encrypted (paired with a code) directly from device to device in the local network.
  • TVBoo Manager (Windows): Encrypted sync in the local network between your PC and your devices, only after one-time pairing.

Error logs (crashes) stay on the device. They are only visible if you use "Export logs" yourself or pair your device with the TVBoo Manager in your local network.

6. Children

TVBoo is not specifically directed at children and collects no data from children. Parental controls (PIN) can lock channels and groups.

7. Your rights

As the developer receives no personal data from you, there is nothing held by the developer to access, correct or delete. You manage all data yourself in the app and on your device. For questions, use the contact above.

8. Changes

If the handling of data changes, this page will be updated. The date above applies.
